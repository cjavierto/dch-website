How billing works

PhilHealth, HMO, and senior or PWD discounts are applied at the billing window before you pay — you do not have to ask for them.

Ward admissions under the PhilHealth case-rate packages carry no balance billing. Private and semi-private rooms are billed with the PhilHealth benefit deducted first.

Every statement is itemised, and the billing desk will walk through each line with you.

About DCH

A family-run community hospital that has cared for southern Cebu since 1974.

Our story

From a six-bed clinic to a 60-bed hospital

Deiparine Community Hospital opened in 1974 as a small lying-in clinic along the national highway in Poblacion, Sibonga. Families who once travelled two hours to Cebu City for a delivery or an appendectomy could finally be treated in their own town.

Five decades later the hospital is DOH-licensed and PhilHealth-accredited, with a 24-hour emergency room, two operating rooms, a delivery suite, and a diagnostic complex that includes digital X-ray, ultrasound, and a 16-slice CT scanner.

Ownership and leadership remain with the Deiparine family, and the majority of the 120-person care team was hired from Sibonga and the neighbouring municipalities of Argao, Dumanjug, and Barili.

Careers / Staff Nurse – Emergency Room

Staff Nurse – Emergency Room

Nursing Service Full-time · rotating shifts 2 openings

The role

You will work in a six-bay emergency room alongside a resident physician and two nursing aides, handling triage, resuscitation, and stabilisation for walk-ins and ambulance arrivals from Sibonga and neighbouring towns.

What you will do

  • Perform triage and initial assessment on arrival, and escalate red-tag cases immediately.
  • Administer medications and IV therapy per physician order, with accurate charting.
  • Assist in minor procedures — suturing, splinting, nebulisation, catheterisation.
  • Prepare patients for admission, transfer, or referral to tertiary hospitals.
  • Endorse cases clearly at shift change and maintain ER supply readiness.

What we are looking for

  • Registered Nurse with an active PRC licence.
  • At least one year of ER, ward, or ICU experience (new graduates with strong internship records are considered).
  • Current BLS certification; ACLS an advantage.
  • Comfortable explaining care in Cebuano and English.
  • Willing to work rotating shifts, weekends, and holidays.

Compensation & benefits

Competitive salary based on experience, night and holiday differentials, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, service incentive leave, free consults for staff and immediate family, and sponsored CPD units.

New 16-slice CT scanner now serving southern Cebu

calendar_monthSeptember 8, 2026 personHospital Information Office schedule3 min read

The new imaging suite on the ground floor, beside the emergency room.

Deiparine Community Hospital has begun operating a 16-slice computed tomography scanner, ending a long-standing need for patients in Sibonga and nearby towns to travel to Cebu City for even routine scans.

The unit is installed in the renovated imaging suite beside the emergency room, which means trauma and stroke cases arriving by ambulance can be scanned within minutes of triage. Head, chest, abdominal, and spine studies are available, with contrast studies scheduled on weekday mornings.

Studies performed before 2 PM are read the same day by our radiologist, and results can be released to the patient or forwarded directly to the referring physician. Emergency scans are read on call, around the clock.

For a stroke or a head injury, the two hours it used to take to reach a scanner in the city were two hours we could not get back. Now the scan happens here.

— Dr. James Deiparine, Medical Director

What patients need to know

  • A physician's request is required for all CT studies, including those ordered from the ER.
  • Fasting of six hours is needed for abdominal studies with contrast.
  • Bring previous X-ray, ultrasound, or CT results if you have them.
  • PhilHealth and accredited HMO coverage may be applied at the billing window before the scan.

Schedule and fees

Non-emergency scans are performed daily from 8 AM to 5 PM by appointment through the imaging desk at (032) 484 1200, local 112. A schedule of fees is posted at the imaging counter and at the billing section.

Imaging & Radiology

Digital X-ray, ultrasound, ECG, and 16-slice CT — read the same day, beside the emergency room.

event_availableSchedule a study stethoscopeMeet the radiologist
Overview

Diagnostics without the trip to the city

The imaging section sits on the ground floor next to the emergency room, so trauma, stroke, and acute abdominal cases can be scanned within minutes of triage. Outpatients are seen by appointment or walk-in, with most plain studies completed in under thirty minutes.

All studies are interpreted by a licensed radiologist. Reports for studies done before 2 PM are released the same day; emergency studies are read on call, twenty-four hours.

Procedures offered

radiologyDigital X-rayChest, skull, spine, extremities
monitor_heart16-slice CT scanHead, chest, abdomen, spine; with or without contrast
pregnant_womanUltrasoundPrenatal, whole abdomen, KUB, thyroid, breast
cardiologyECG & 2D-echoResting ECG daily; 2D-echo Tue & Fri

How to prepare

  • Bring a physician's request — required for every study.
  • Fast for six hours before an abdominal ultrasound or a contrast CT.
  • For pelvic ultrasound, drink water and keep a full bladder.
  • Tell the technologist if you are pregnant or allergic to contrast dye.
  • Bring any previous imaging results for comparison.
