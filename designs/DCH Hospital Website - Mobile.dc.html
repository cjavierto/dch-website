Overview

Diagnostics without the trip to the city

The imaging section sits beside the emergency room, so trauma and stroke cases can be scanned within minutes of triage. Most plain studies finish in under thirty minutes.

All studies are read by a licensed radiologist — same day before 2 PM, on call for emergencies.

New 16-slice CT scanner now serving southern Cebu

calendar_monthSeptember 8, 2026 schedule3 min read

The new imaging suite on the ground floor, beside the emergency room.

Deiparine Community Hospital has begun operating a 16-slice computed tomography scanner, ending a long-standing need for patients in Sibonga to travel to Cebu City for routine scans.

The unit is installed in the renovated imaging suite beside the emergency room, so trauma and stroke cases arriving by ambulance can be scanned within minutes of triage. Head, chest, abdominal, and spine studies are available, with contrast studies scheduled on weekday mornings.

Studies performed before 2 PM are read the same day by our radiologist, and results can be released to the patient or forwarded to the referring physician.

For a stroke or a head injury, the two hours it used to take to reach a scanner in the city were two hours we could not get back. Now the scan happens here.

— Dr. James Deiparine, Medical Director

What patients need to know

  • A physician's request is required for all CT studies.
  • Fasting of six hours is needed for abdominal studies with contrast.
  • Bring previous X-ray, ultrasound, or CT results if you have them.
  • PhilHealth and HMO coverage may be applied at the billing window.

Schedule and fees

Non-emergency scans are performed daily from 8 AM to 5 PM by appointment through the imaging desk at (032) 484 1200, local 112.

About DCH

A family-run community hospital caring for southern Cebu since 1974.

Our story

From a six-bed clinic to a 60-bed hospital

DCH opened in 1974 as a small lying-in clinic along the national highway in Poblacion, Sibonga. Families who once travelled two hours to Cebu City for a delivery or an appendectomy could finally be treated in their own town.

Five decades later the hospital is DOH-licensed and PhilHealth-accredited, with a 24-hour emergency room, two operating rooms, a delivery suite, and a diagnostic complex.

Leadership remains with the Deiparine family, and most of the 120-person care team was hired from Sibonga and neighbouring towns.

Staff Nurse – Emergency Room

Nursing Service Rotating shifts 2 openings

The role

You will work in a six-bay emergency room alongside a resident physician and two nursing aides, handling triage, resuscitation, and stabilisation for walk-ins and ambulance arrivals.

What you will do

  • Perform triage on arrival and escalate red-tag cases immediately.
  • Administer medications and IV therapy per physician order.
  • Assist in minor procedures — suturing, splinting, nebulisation.
  • Prepare patients for admission, transfer, or referral.
  • Endorse cases clearly at shift change.

What we are looking for

  • Registered Nurse with an active PRC licence.
  • At least one year of ER, ward, or ICU experience.
  • Current BLS certification; ACLS an advantage.
  • Comfortable explaining care in Cebuano and English.

Compensation & benefits

Competitive salary based on experience, night and holiday differentials, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, service incentive leave, free consults for staff and immediate family, and sponsored CPD units.
